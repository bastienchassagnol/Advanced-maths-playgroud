## Caption

There is no single best method for matrix inversion—the right choice depends on the matrix structure and application.

* Gauss–Jordan: direct and educational
* Adjugate: useful for small symbolic matrices
* LU: efficient for repeated linear solves
* QR: stable orthogonal factorization
* Cholesky: optimized for symmetric positive-definite matrices
* SVD: handles rank deficiency and pseudoinverses

In numerical computing, explicitly forming A^{-1} is often unnecessary. Solving Ax=b through a suitable factorization is usually faster and more stable.
