## Caption

Vector multiplication is not a single operation—the symbol determines the calculation, geometric meaning, and type of output.

• Scalar multiplication changes magnitude or direction.
• The dot product measures alignment and produces a scalar.
• The cross product produces a perpendicular vector in 3D.
• The outer product constructs a matrix.
• The Hadamard product multiplies corresponding components.
• The wedge product creates a bivector representing oriented area.
• The geometric product combines scalar alignment and oriented area:
[
\mathbf{ab}=\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b+\mathbf a\wedge\mathbf b
]
• The scalar triple product measures signed volume.
• The vector triple product produces a vector in the plane of (\mathbf b) and (\mathbf c).

Scalar, vector, matrix, bivector, or multivector—the chosen product determines the result.
