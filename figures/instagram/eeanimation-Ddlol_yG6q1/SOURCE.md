## Caption

Dynamical systems study how a system’s state evolves over time.

Continuous-time systems: ẋ = f(x,t)
Discrete-time systems: xₖ₊₁ = F(xₖ)

The field connects state-space flows, equilibria, stability, linearization, eigenvalues, phase portraits, limit cycles, Lyapunov methods, bifurcations, invariant manifolds, Poincaré maps, attractors, chaos, and fractals.

These tools reveal whether trajectories converge, oscillate, diverge, reorganize as parameters change, or become highly sensitive to initial conditions—often indicated by λₘₐₓ > 0.

Applications include control systems, electronic circuits, mechanics, biology, climate science, and complex networks.
